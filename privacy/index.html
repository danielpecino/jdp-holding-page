Privacy

Home / Privacy

JDP Security Ltd is responsible for the personal information you give us through this website, by email or by phone. Email info@jdpsecurity.co.uk if you want to see it, correct it, or ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • If you email or phone us, we receive your contact details and message, usually your name, email address, phone number, site address, existing system and what you need.
  • If you send the enquiry form, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), site address, existing system and how we can help.
  • If you click "Accept all" on our cookie banner, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the website: pages viewed, roughly where you are (town or region), your type of device and browser, how you found us, and whether you tapped our phone number, clicked our email address or sent the form. It does not give us your name or contact details. If you click "Reject all", none of this is collected.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, give you a quote and do the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps you asked for before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (Google Analytics). Our lawful basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not keep a marketing list from this website.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms (our form service): when you send the enquiry form, what you type is passed to Web3Forms, which emails it to us at info@jdpsecurity.co.uk. We use it only to reply to your enquiry.
  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager), only if you click "Accept all". Google may process this information outside the UK under safeguards approved for UK data. We have not turned on Google advertising features, and we have not allowed Google to use this analytics data for its own products and services. Data is kept for 14 months.
  • Zoho Mail (our email provider) stores the emails we receive.
  • Render keeps short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the website running and secure.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiry emails and form messages: as long as we need them to reply and do the work, and for any records we must keep by law.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it where we no longer need it. You can also object to how we use it, and withdraw your cookie consent at any time. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are not satisfied.

Changes

We will update this page if we change how we use your information. Last updated: 11 November 2026.
