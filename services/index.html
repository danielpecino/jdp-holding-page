Maintenance & Reactive Support

We provide planned maintenance, fault finding and call-outs for existing fire and security systems. We work with systems already in place, keeping equipment where it can be safely maintained and advising when replacement is genuinely required.

Request a quote

Fire Detection & Alarm Systems

We design, install, commission and take over existing fire alarm systems, including detection, sounders, interfaces and cause-and-effect configurations tailored to your building.

We work to the relevant British Standards for the premises, including BS 5839-1 for commercial buildings and BS 5839-6 for dwellings, with systems fully commissioned before handover.

Request a quote

CCTV Systems

We design CCTV systems around your site, taking into account entrances, yards, work areas and other key locations to ensure the right camera coverage and recording solution.

Where possible, we work with your existing equipment, advising whether cameras can be retained or if the recording system needs upgrading.

Request a quote

Intruder Alarms

We install and maintain wired and wireless intruder alarm systems, with options including keypads, app control and remote signalling to suit your requirements.

Where an existing system can be retained, we’ll advise you accordingly. We can also arrange alarm signalling to you, your nominated keyholders or an alarm receiving centre.

Request a quote

Access Control

We install access control systems including readers, fobs, door controllers and intercoms, from single doors to multi-door systems.

We assess the door and locking hardware first, advising if a lock, closer or door issue needs to be addressed before access control is installed.
