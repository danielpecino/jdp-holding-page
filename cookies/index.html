Cookies

Home / Cookies

This page explains the cookies and similar technology used on jdpsecurity.co.uk, and how you can change your choice at any time.

Your choice

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept cookies. Analytics cookies are set only if you click "Accept all". If you click "Reject all", no analytics or advertising cookies are set, and the website works exactly the same.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

What we always use

This is needed for the website to work, so it does not need your consent.

NameWhat it doesHow long
jdp_consent_v1 (kept in your browser's storage, not a cookie, and not sent to us)Remembers whether you clicked "Accept all" or "Reject all", so we don't ask on every page12 months, then we ask again

Cookies we only use if you click "Accept all"

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits, see which pages are used, and see when someone taps our phone number, clicks our email address or sends the enquiry form. This helps us improve the website. Google Analytics does not tell us who you are.

NameSet byWhat it doesHow long
_gaGoogle AnalyticsTells one visitor apart from another, using a random number (not your name)2 years
_ga_13DCFXRNFWGoogle AnalyticsKeeps track of your current visit (session)2 years

Your browser may delete these sooner. Google Tag Manager is only loaded after you click "Accept all", and it does not set cookies of its own. If you click "Reject all", or change your choice to reject later, we remove these cookies.

Google's own information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies and https://policies.google.com/privacy

The enquiry form

The enquiry form does not set any cookies. What you type is sent to our form service, Web3Forms, which emails it to us at info@jdpsecurity.co.uk (see our Privacy page).

Blocking cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website will still work.
